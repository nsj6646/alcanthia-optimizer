# Alcanthia shop sales and storage: research note

Checked 2026-11-01 UTC / 2026-11-02 KST. Public official web client and its embedded guides/patches only; no account, gameplay, or authenticated API. [Game page][game] → [bootstrap][boot] → [client bundle][client], SHA-256 `827845f680707a23f9cc6afb6c8f50767f20bb1247df4e5f5c1790e111ef6b5e`. Identifiers below are bundle-specific. Client behavior is **not** independent proof of server enforcement.

## Verified in official client and announcements

- **NPC shop:** `Wwe=1000`; `Vwe` accepts one item key and an integer quantity 1–1,000 per sale action. `YWe.buildSellDetail` offers 1/10/100/1,000 buttons, the last when 1,000 are sellable. This is not a daily or inventory-wide cap in the examined action. Different items require separate observed actions; no atomic multi-item collective limit was established. The 2026-04-23 patch describes the 1,000-unit buttons. [Client: `Wwe`, `Vwe`, `YWe.buildSellDetail`, patch `2026-04-23T23:55:00+09:00`][client]
- **Mode matters:** the same detail screen also has market instant-sale quick buttons up to 1,000 per click (`buildMarketSellButtons`), but they invoke the separate market `sell` path. Market order screen `buildPlaceUI` allows up to `ft(state)` per order, subject to sellable stock; instant mode is also bounded by matching buy orders at the quoted price. Listing mode has an additional 10× capacity limit on existing plus newly listed quantity (`dAe`, `qk`). Official 2026-05-01 patch says market order quantity changed from 1,000 to inventory capacity; 2026-07-13 patch mentions the instant-sale buy-order limit. [Client: identifiers and dated patches][client]
- **Inventory:** `using_space_knot` says base 1,000 per matching slot (same kind and enhancement, etc.), not total inventory, and +500 per correctly staged knot per slot. `ft` calculates `1000 + 500 × inventoryCapLevel`, or `floor(1.5 × that)` in `sleeping_roots` (잠든 뿌리); its zone description also states +50%. `Jt` counts matching inventory plus brewing ingredients. A 4,000 limit is therefore plausible per slot after six expansions, but is not a universal cap; that example becomes 6,000 in the zone. Separate warehouse/chest limits were not established. [Client: `using_space_knot`, `ft`, `Jt`, `sleeping_roots`][client]
- **Shop price and reset:** official 2026-06-15 12:18 KST patch says same-item shop sales decrease price 1% per 1,000 sold that day, resetting at 09:00. `wi` uses UTC day boundaries, equivalent to 09:00 KST; `NW` reads a previous-day count as zero. `Vwe` indexes `shopSellRecords` by the full key; `_e` includes code, enhancement and applicable engraved data/flags. This shop path rejects bound, untradable and bundled items. Different eligible enhancement keys thus have separate **client** counters; market `dAe` does not update those records. [Client: dated patch, `wi`, `NW`, `_e`, `Xt`, `Vwe`, `dAe`][client]
- **Batch boundary and rounding:** for pre-sale count `q`, quantity `n`, and enhancement-adjusted base price `P`, `KW`/`YW` compute `n × max(1, ceil(P × (1 − 0.01 × floor(q/1000))))`; `Vwe` then adds `n` to the count. The starting unit price applies to the **whole action**, even across a threshold; reduction is linear from base, with unit-price ceiling and minimum 1. Example: +0 `star_crystal` has `P=200`; at `q=999`, one 1,000 sale yields 200,000 G, but selling 1 then the remaining 999 through available buttons yields 200 + 999×198 = 198,002 G. For base 20, `ceil(20×0.99)=20`, so the first tier need not change the unit price. These are client calculations, not observed payouts. [Client: `Kve.sell`, `Xp`, `KW`, `YW`, `Vwe`][client]

## Inference, discrepancy, and unknowns

- **Planning inference:** model NPC-shop actions individually by key and starting counter, without splitting an action across price tiers; model market separately and capacity by expansion/zone. [Client: `Vwe`, `ft`, `buildPlaceUI`][client]
- **Documentation/code discrepancy:** beginner `inventory_cap_warning` says same-kind/same-enhancement items are limited to 1,000 without mentioning upgrades or zone bonus; `using_space_knot` and `ft` qualify it. The 1,000 market quick button and later capacity-based market order patch concern different paths. [Client: these identifiers, `buildMarketSellButtons`, `buildPlaceUI`, 2026-05-01 patch][client]
- **Unanswered:** actual server validation/reconciliation, payouts, reset timing, cross-enhancement aggregation, market-to-shop counter interaction, and individual players' capacities. The [prior note][prior] did not settle batch-boundary behavior or mention the zone modifier; this note resolves those only for the retrieved client.
- **Check performed:** extracted `wi`, `NW`, `KW`, `YW`, and `ft` from the bundle and ran synthetic Node assertions: 200,000 vs 198,002 boundary totals; base-20 prices 20 at 1,000 and 19 at 5,000; capacity 4,000/6,000; reset 00:00 UTC. No live transaction.
